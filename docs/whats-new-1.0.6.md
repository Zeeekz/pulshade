# What's New in 1.0.6 (build 23)

For the App Store "What's New" box and the TestFlight notes. Plain words, no internals.

- Message anyone by their username. Find someone and tap Message: no friend request needed. Chats from people who
  aren't in your contacts wait under Message requests until you reply, and they don't see that you've read them.
  Prefer the old way? Settings → Privacy → Who can message me → Only people I accept.
- Contacts are your choice. Add someone you chat with to your contacts whenever you like.
- Adding a friend to a group or channel puts them straight in. No more waiting for them to accept.
- New channel followers see what was posted before they joined (the last 100 posts, with photos and videos).
- Profiles: one simple card everywhere and one Edit profile page.
- Fixes for group messages that could arrive out of order.
